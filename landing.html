Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

UkayFinds is a student project: a free directory and community for ukay-ukay stalls in Cebu. We keep this short and plain. There are no payments on UkayFinds, so we never collect card or bank details.

What we store

  • Your account: username, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We can't read your password.
  • Your profile: display name, bio and location text, only if you fill them in.
  • What you post: hauls and their photos, comments, ratings and reviews, stalls you suggest, stalls and hauls you save, and your Ukay Trail stamps and rewards.
  • Notifications sent to you (likes, comments, ratings, new stalls near you).
  • Reports you send about a post or comment.

Your location

  • We only ask for your location through your browser's permission prompt, and you can say no. Find a Stall still works without it, just without distances.
  • When you allow it, we use it to sort stalls by distance, to draw directions, and to check you are at a stall when you collect a Ukay Trail stamp. The stamp check happens on our server and the exact position is not kept.
  • To tell you when a new stall opens near you, we keep one rounded position (about 100 m accuracy) for your account. It is replaced each time you share a new one.

Why we use it

Only to run the site: sign you in, show your posts, keep the community safe (for example, limiting spam and checking suggested stalls), and send notifications. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Who can see what

  • Your username, posts, comments and public ratings are visible to other users. A rating posted as anonymous shows as “Anonymous thrifter”.
  • Your email address and password are never shown to other users.
  • Site admins can see account and moderation information so they can review reports and suggested stalls.

Cookies

We use one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for the site to work, and it is removed when you sign out or close your browser. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

Maps and directions load from OpenStreetMap-based services (map tiles and routing), and fonts load from Google Fonts. These services can see your IP address when your browser loads them.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are hashed, the site is served over HTTPS, and sign-in is limited after repeated wrong passwords. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Deleting your data

You can change your details on your profile at any time. To delete your account and the posts, ratings, stamps and photos linked to it, email us at the address below from the email you signed up with, and we will delete them.

Children

UkayFinds is meant for people 13 and older.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL

This policy may change as the site grows. When it does, we will update the date at the top.

Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026

By creating an account or using UkayFinds you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

What UkayFinds is

UkayFinds is a free directory and community that helps people find ukay-ukay stalls in Cebu and share their hauls. It is not a shop. We don't sell, hold or deliver anything, and we don't process payments. Any buying or selling happens in person at the stall, between you and the seller.

Your account

  • You must give a real email address and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • One person, one account. Don't pretend to be someone else.

What you post

  • You keep ownership of your photos and words. By posting them you let UkayFinds show them on the site.
  • Only post photos you took or have permission to use, and don't include other people's faces or private details without their OK.
  • Don't post spam, scams, hateful or sexual content, or anything illegal.
  • Reviews should be honest and based on your own visit. No fake or paid reviews.

Suggesting stalls

When you suggest a stall it appears on the map right away as “Unverified” until an admin checks it. Only suggest real stalls with the right pin. Fake or duplicate stalls may be removed or merged.

Ukay Trail

Stamps are given only when you are at the stall. Trying to fake your location, or stamping a stall you did not visit, can lead to your stamps and rewards being removed. Rewards depend on the stalls taking part and can change or end without notice.

Moderation

Admins can remove posts, comments, ratings or stalls that break these terms, and can suspend or delete accounts that keep doing so. You can report a post or comment from its menu.

No guarantees

Stall details like hours, prices and locations come from the community and may be out of date or wrong. Check before you travel. UkayFinds is provided “as is”, and we are not responsible for the stalls, the items they sell, or what happens between buyers and sellers.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date above when we do. Questions: YOUR-CONTACT-EMAIL
